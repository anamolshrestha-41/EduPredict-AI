## ABOUT THE PROJECT : EDUPREDICT AI

- PROBLEM STATEMENT
  ---
  Most Schools and Colleges need to identify students who may struggle academically. Teachers can use information such as previous grades, study time, attendence related information, and past failures to understand student performance.
  The problem is thatreviewing every student's information manually can take time, student who need support may not to be identified early enough.
  ---

- SOLUTION
  ---
  Buid a machine learning system that predicts student's final grades, estimates their likelihood of passing, compares multiple ML algorithms, and identifies patterns associated with acedemic performance.
  ---

- OBJECTIVES
  ---
  i. Data loading, cleaning, and exploratory data analysis.
  ii. Data visualization and feature relationships.
  iii. Regression and classification.
  iv. Training and testing machine learning models.
  v. Feature scaling and preprocessing pipelines.
  vi. Model evaluation and comparison.
  vii. Cross-validation and hyperparameter tuning.
  viii. Feature importance and model interpretation.
  ix. Making predictions for new student records.
  x. Saving a trained model.
  xi. Writing a professional README and publishing the project on GitHub.
  ---

- What We USE
  ---
  The system will use two types of machine learning:
  i. Regression: Predict a student's final grade
  ii. Classification: Predict whether a student is likely to pass or fail.
  ---

- NOTE:
  This project is for learning and decision support. Its predictions should not be used alone to make important decisions about real students.
